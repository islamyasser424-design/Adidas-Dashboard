An interactive Business Intelligence project built using Power BI to analyze global sales performance, track financial metrics, and uncover regional insights for strategic decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Structure & Core Highlights
The dashboard is structured into three main pages balancing high-level overviews, detailed analysis, and seamless navigation:

1. **Home Page:**
   * Interactive landing page featuring clean navigation buttons for smooth transitions between dashboard sections.
   
2. **Overview Page:**
   * **KPIs:** Tracked Total Sales ($77.55M), Profit ($999.49K), Cost ($76.55M), and Total Orders (4K).
   * **Sales Per Month:** Line chart illustrating the sales trajectory over time (2017 – 2020).
   * **Sales by Category:** Breakdown across core product categories such as Bikes, Clothing, and Components.
   * **Sales by Country:** Geographic distribution of sales across key markets.
   * **Top 10 SubCategories:** Highlighted top-performing sub-categories like Road Bikes and Mountain Bikes.

3. **Analysis Page:**
   * **Operational Metrics:** Average Sales ($1.34K), Quantity Sold (204K), and Total Resellers (701).
   * **Top 10 Sales Persons:** Individual sales performance evaluation to align revenue with team contributions.
   * **Regional & Reseller Insights:** Analysis of sales per region and reseller distribution by country region.
   * **Top 10 Sales Products:** Identification of top revenue-generating products to guide inventory management and marketing strategies.

## 🗄️ Data Modeling & Architecture
* **Star Schema Design:** Built a well-structured relational model with a central `Sales` Fact Table connected to key dimension tables (`Product`, `Reseller`, `Salesperson`, `Region`, `Targets`, and `SalespersonRegion`) ensuring robust and scalable performance.

## ⚙️ Key Features & Interactivity
* **Seamless Navigation:** Designed a dedicated home page with navigation buttons for smooth transitions between dashboard pages.
* **Dynamic Filtering:** Cross-filtering across all visual elements using interactive slicers and date sliders for real-time analysis.
